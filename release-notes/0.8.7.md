# bubo 0.8.7

This release makes lessons teach instead of giving answers away, flips
bilingual replies so Portuguese comes first, and lets the mascot announce
updates in its own voice.

## What's new

- **bubo announces updates**: when a newer version is available and bubo is
  idle, it opens a speech bubble with the version and the install and later
  actions, instead of leaving the news only in the bottom banner.
- **Beginner replies stay on topic**: bubo answers what the learner actually
  sent before offering to teach related words or keep talking, instead of
  switching to an unrelated drill.
- **Lessons with a second chance**: the first miss shows a hint without the
  answer; the second miss (or a reveal) shows the answer with a longer
  explanation and a sad bubo. A correct answer shows a proud bubo.
- **No more answers in the examples**: lesson bodies and examples were
  rewritten so they illustrate the rule without containing the full exercise
  answer.
- **Lessons through B2**: present perfect, conditionals and passive voice join
  the catalog, and the lessons entry point is available at every level.
- **Portuguese-first bilingual replies**: in English + Portuguese mode bubo
  writes Portuguese first and keeps the English behind a "ver em inglês"
  button in the full, compact and popup views.

## Upgrade

Nothing to do. Settings → Atualizações offers 0.8.7 like any other version.
