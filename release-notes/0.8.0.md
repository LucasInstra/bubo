# bubo 0.8.0

This release adds a beginner mode for A1/A2: the tutor waits for a guess before
showing the translation, lets you choose a grammar focus, and includes seven
short guided lessons.

## What's new

- **Translation challenge (A1/A2)**: bilingual replies keep the Portuguese line behind a `ver tradução` button, so you try to understand the English first. A setting turns the challenge on or off.
- **Hint before the answer**: at A1/A2, bubo gives a short hint (first word, tense or related word) before the full answer. In Portuguese-only mode it also invites you to guess an English phrase before translating it.
- **Study focus (A1/A2)**: choose free chat, verb to be, present simple, past simple, questions, articles, prepositions or everyday vocabulary. The focus shapes the conversation and the correction pass, and a small bar shows the active focus above the composer.
- **Seven mini-lessons**: verb to be, present simple, past simple, questions, articles and plural, prepositions, and everyday vocabulary. Each lesson has three short steps with an explanation, examples and one exercise.
- **Practice integration**: a wrong lesson answer becomes a card in the same spaced-repetition queue as a chat correction. Lesson progress is stored in the local database.
- **Practice from the lesson**: finishing a lesson can set its grammar focus for the next conversation.
- **Works without an API key**: the mini-lessons are local and do not call the model.

## Upgrade

Existing settings files keep working. A1/A2 gets the translation challenge on by default and a free study focus; B1 and above are unchanged. Lesson progress starts empty and is stored in the existing SQLite database.
