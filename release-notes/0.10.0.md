# bubo 0.10.0

This release closes the audit fixes from 0.9.1 and the bugs that showed up
after it: reading old replies, keeping deletions honest, the first run, updates
outside the chat, accessibility, and an English nudge that no longer repeats
what the learner already wrote.

## What's new

- **Saved bilingual replies**: a conversation stays readable when you change
  the reply language. The body follows the setting, the other language stays
  behind the reveal, and no raw `EN:` or `PT:` line is left in the text.
- **Translation challenge**: compact and popup now hide the Portuguese half of
  older replies behind the same reveal as the full chat.
- **Corrections and practice**: answer checking compares whole words, a wrong
  lesson sentence saves the full corrected sentence, and a recurring mistake is
  due right away instead of weeks ahead.
- **Deleting**: removing a message or a whole conversation also removes the
  corrections it produced, including their copy on other messages, so a deleted
  card no longer comes back on the next start.
- **First run**: the setup wizard can be dismissed, so history, practice,
  settings and backup stay reachable before a key is connected.
- **Updates**: the wizard, compact and popup announce an available build, and a
  failed install is visible there instead of only in Settings.
- **No English echo**: bubo no longer asks you to say in English what you just
  wrote in English, and never hands your own sentence back as a target. The
  nudge only appears after a Portuguese message, with a one to three word
  starter.
- **Accessibility and saved state**: new messages are announced, the composer
  and practice input have stable labels, selection chips show their state, and
  Escape while renaming no longer closes history. The pin no longer reverts when
  you save settings, and a correct lesson step is saved before you advance.
